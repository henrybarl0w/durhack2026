      How it works

      Every legal harmonisation of a Bach tune is a different number.

      1 · Choose

      Pick up to three words from a list of 30,000, or type any short text. It becomes a single number.

      2 · Compose

      At each beat the engine offers up to four chords that follow the rules of Bach chorale writing. The number decides which one is written, beat after beat.

      3 · Decipher

      Reading the chords back, and knowing the choices that were open at each beat, gives the number, and the number gives the words.

      The intended flow is to use Scan: point the camera at a printed page and let the app read it. MusicXML and WAV files are provided as extra outputs for inspection and sharing, while the page also demonstrates the intermediate steps in the process. Error correction adds a 16-bit checksum, so up to two misread chords can be repaired, and the fancy style writes the same kind of message with smoother lines, a walking bass and ornaments.
      Chorales from the Riemenschneider collection, via the music21 corpus.
